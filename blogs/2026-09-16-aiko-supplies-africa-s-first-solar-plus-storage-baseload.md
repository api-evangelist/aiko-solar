The Kamoa 233MW solar project, developed, owned, and operated by CrossBoundary Energy to supply clean electricity to the Kamoa-Kakula Copper Mining Complex, has been commissioned in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and entered full commercial operation on August 12, 2026.
